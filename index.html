Altera Terra — three new directions

Prototypes D, E and F, each a complete Home / Journey / Enquire set, distinct from the three directions already explored (A · Quiet Luxury, B · Nocturne, C · Héritage).

D · Atelier

Warm, artisanal, notebook-bound. Olive, sand and terracotta; Fraunces + Work Sans.

E · Cinéma

Almost no text, full-bleed scenes, a slow cinematic scroll. Near-black, ivory, brass.

F · Carnet de Voyage

A travel-magazine feature: cover, columns, captions. Newsprint, ink-blue, rouge.
