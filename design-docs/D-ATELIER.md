# D · Atelier

**Concept.** An artist's studio ledger, not a brochure. The site reads like a bound atelier notebook: warm paper, a serif with real character, every section introduced by a small hand-set numeral as if tipped in by a letterpress. Nothing glossy — everything held, worked, considered.

**Palette** (roles, not just hexes)
- `#F6F1E7` Paper — page background
- `#5B5A3F` Olive — primary ink for headings, nav
- `#C98756` Terracotta — accent, links, the one warm CTA
- `#2E2A22` Umber — body text
- `#D8CDB8` Sand — hairlines, card borders, dividers

**Typography.** `Fraunces` (serif, display — high-contrast, inky, slightly irregular) for headings; `Work Sans` for body and UI. Headings set large and loose; body set narrow-measure for a reading, not scanning, rhythm.

**Recurring motif.** A thin hand-drawn-feeling rule (`border-style: solid`, 1px, Sand) under every section numeral, and a small tipped-in "plate" frame (double border, generous padding) around every photograph — like a mounted print in a notebook, never a cropped card.

**Image style.** Warm, available light. Textures and hands-on detail over wide establishing shots: a garden walkway, a sunset acacia, a whitewashed wall. No drone shots, no gloss.

**Three key decisions.**
1. Sections are numbered (I, II, III…) in small caps Olive, standing in for icons — satisfies "no generic library icons" while giving the eye a wayfinding rhythm.
2. The hero is quiet: a single image at 70vh with the lema set small and left, not shouting across the fold — the opposite of a hero banner.
3. The nav is a vertical word-mark on desktop (fixed left rail at wide widths) collapsing to a simple top bar + hamburger below 960px, reinforcing the "notebook margin" feeling without a sidebar on mobile.
